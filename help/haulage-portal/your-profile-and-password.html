Chapter 7

Your Profile and Password

7.1 Updating your details#

Click Profile in the menu, or click your name at the top right and then “Profile.” Change your Full Name, Email or Phone, then click “Save Changes.”

Note

Your email address is also what you sign in with. If you change it, sign in with the new address from then on. An email address can only belong to one portal login.

7.2 Changing your password#

  1. Open your Profile.
  2. Under Change Password, enter your Current Password.
  3. Enter the new password in New Password and again in Confirm New Password.
  4. Click “Update Password.”

A password needs at least 8 characters, with upper and lower case letters and at least one number.

7.3 Keeping your account safe#

  • Don’t share your password. Ask your transport provider for a login for each colleague who needs one.
  • Log out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
  • When someone leaves your company, ask your transport provider to switch off their login.
  • If you think someone else knows your password, sign in and change it straight away. Signing in also signs out anyone else using your login.

Chapter 4

Trips

A trip is the job itself: a truck and a driver carrying your cargo. Your transport provider creates a trip when they book an accepted request, or directly for work you arranged with them another way, such as by phone. All of your company’s trips are under My Trips.

4.1 Your trips list#

My Trips lists your trips, newest first, with the trip number (such as TRP-2026-0001), the route, the truck’s number plate, the driver and the status. Click “View” to open a trip.

4.2 Trip stages#

StatusWhat it means
AssignedA truck and a driver have been booked for your cargo
LoadingThe truck is being loaded
En RouteThe truck has left and is on its way to the destination
DeliveredThe cargo has arrived at the destination
ReturningThe truck is on its way back after delivering
CompletedThe trip is finished
CancelledThe trip was called off. The trip page shows the reason.

After Delivered, a trip goes either straight to Completed, or to Returning and then Completed.

Note

Your transport provider’s staff update the stage as the trip goes, so it can be a little behind events on the road. Reload the page to see the latest. For an urgent update, call the driver or your transport provider.

4.3 The trip page#

The trip page shows:

  • Truck: the number plate of the truck carrying your cargo
  • Driver: the driver’s name and phone number, for example to arrange access at the delivery point
  • Route and Cargo, with the weight
  • Pickup: the pickup time your transport provider planned, if they set one
  • Departure, Arrival and Completed: the times the trip reached each of those stages
  • Cancellation Reason, if the trip was cancelled
  • Timeline: each change of stage, with the date and time and any comment from your transport provider

Note

If a truck breaks down or a driver can’t continue, your transport provider may change them during the trip. The trip page always shows the current truck and driver.

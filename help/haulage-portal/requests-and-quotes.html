Chapter 3

Requests and Quotes

A transport request is how you ask your transport provider to move something: what it is, where from, where to, and when. They reply with a quote (a price). When you accept it, they book the trip.

3.1 How a request moves#

StatusWhat it meansWhat happens next
PendingSent, waiting for a quoteYour transport provider prices it. To change or withdraw it, contact them.
QuotedYour transport provider has sent a priceAccept or reject the quote (sections 3.4 and 3.5)
AcceptedYou agreed to the latest quoteYour transport provider books the truck and driver
RejectedTurned down, by you or by your transport providerThe request page shows the reason. Your transport provider can reopen it.
ConvertedThe trip has been bookedFollow the trip under My Trips (Chapter 4)

3.2 Sending a request#

  1. Click My Requests in the menu, then “New Request.”
  2. Choose the Service Type if you know which service you need. You can leave it blank.
  3. Choose the Origin (where the cargo is collected) and the Destination (where it goes). They must be different places.
  4. Enter the Cargo Description, for example “20 pallets of bottled water.” This is the only other field you must fill in.
  5. If you can, enter the Weight (tons) and the Preferred Pickup Date, and add any Special Instructions, such as loading hours, a contact person on site, or access restrictions.
  6. Click “Submit Request.”

The request gets a number such as REQ-2026-0001 and starts as Pending. Quote that number whenever you contact your transport provider about it.

Note

The Origin and Destination lists only show places your transport provider has set up. If yours is missing, ask them to add it before you send the request.

Note

You can’t edit or withdraw a request in the portal once it is sent. Contact your transport provider: they can change a request while it is still Pending.

3.3 Reviewing a quote#

When your transport provider prices your request, its status changes to Quoted and it appears under “Quotes Awaiting Your Action” on the Dashboard. Click “Review” there, or open the request from My Requests. The Quote section shows:

  • Quote: the quote number, such as QUO-2026-0001. The newest one is marked “Latest.”
  • Valid Until: the last day the price is on offer.
  • Amount: the price and its currency.

If your transport provider revises the price, the new quote appears at the top marked “Latest,” and the earlier ones stay below for your records. Accepting a request always accepts its latest quote.

Note

The portal doesn’t send emails or alerts. Check the Dashboard for new quotes, or ask your transport provider to let you know when a quote is ready.

Note

If the Valid Until date has passed, contact your transport provider to confirm the price before you accept.

3.4 Accepting a quote#

  1. Open the request.
  2. Check the request details and the latest quote: its amount, currency and Valid Until date.
  3. Click “Accept Quote,” then confirm.

The request becomes Accepted, and its Timeline shows “Accepted by client via portal.” Your transport provider then books a truck and driver. When they do, the request becomes Converted and the trip appears under My Trips.

Caution

You can’t undo an acceptance in the portal. If you accepted by mistake, contact your transport provider straight away.

3.5 Rejecting a quote#

  1. Open the request.
  2. Click “Reject Quote.”
  3. Add a Comment saying why, for example “Price too high” or “No longer needed.” It is optional, but your transport provider sees it.
  4. Click “Reject.”

Rejecting a quote rejects the whole request, not just that price. If you would like a different price or date, say so in the comment: your transport provider can reopen the request and send a new quote. Otherwise, send a new request when you are ready.

3.6 Following a request#

My Requests lists all of your company’s requests, newest first, with the route, cargo, status and the date each was sent. Click “View” to open one. The request page shows:

  • The details you sent: route, service type, cargo, weight, preferred pickup date and special instructions
  • The Rejection Reason, if the request was rejected
  • The quotes (section 3.3)
  • The Timeline: each change of status, with the date and time and any comment, such as the reason for a rejection

Chapter 11

Accounts and Cash Book

Who can do this

Admin and Finance, under Finance → Accounts.

11.1 Cash, mobile money and bank accounts#

Accounts are where your money is: office cash, mobile money wallets and bank accounts. Every payment received, expense paid, transfer and bank charge moves an account’s balance, and each account keeps a full ledger. All balances are kept in cedis (GHS).

A new system starts with three accounts, Office Cash, MTN Mobile Money and Main Bank Account, all with a zero balance. Edit them to match your real accounts before you start.

The Accounts page shows each account with its balance, and the Total Balance of all active accounts. One account of each type can be the default, which is picked first on forms.

11.2 Adding an account#

  1. Finance → Accounts → “New Account.”
  2. Enter the Account Name and choose the Type: Cash, Mobile Money or Bank.
  3. For Mobile Money, choose the Network (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo) and enter the Number and Registered Name. For Bank, enter the Bank Name and Account Number, and optionally the Branch, Account Name and SWIFT Code.
  4. Enter the Opening Balance (GHS): what the account holds today.
  5. Tick “Set as default for this type” if it should be picked first on forms.
  6. Click “Create Account.”

Note

After an account is created, you can change its name, notes, default and active status, but not its type or opening balance.

11.3 The account page and ledger#

Open an account to see its Current Balance; This Month In, Out and Net; and the Transaction Ledger: every credit and debit, with the balance after each one.

11.4 Moving money between accounts#

  1. Finance → Accounts → “Transfer.”
  2. Choose the From Account and the To Account. The form shows each one’s balance.
  3. Enter the Amount (GHS), the Date and a Description.
  4. If the transfer cost a fee, open “Transfer Charges (optional),” choose the Charge Account the fee came out of, and enter the Transfer Charge (GHS), and any Other Charges (GHS), with descriptions.
  5. Click “Execute Transfer.” It gets a reference such as TRF-2026-0001.

11.5 Recording a bank charge#

For a charge that isn’t part of a transfer, such as a monthly bank fee, open the account and click “+ Record Bank Charge.” Enter the Description, Amount (GHS) and Date, then click “Save Charge.”

11.6 Deactivating or deleting an account#

An account can only be deleted if its balance is zero and it has no transactions. Otherwise, deactivate it from its Edit page: it stops appearing on forms and its history stays.

11.7 Currencies and exchange rates#

Who can do this

Admin and Finance, under Finance → Currencies.

The currency list holds the currencies staff can choose for clients, quotes, trips, expenses, invoices, proformas and payments, and their exchange rates to cedis. It starts with GHS, USD, EUR and NGN; add others as you need them.

ColumnWhat it means
CodeThe 3-letter international code, e.g. USD
NameThe currency’s name, e.g. US Dollar
Today's RateHow many cedis one unit is worth today, e.g. “1 USD = GHS 15.2500,” or “Not set”
SinceThe date today’s rate applies from
StatusActive currencies appear on forms; inactive ones don’t

Rates are kept with the date they apply from, so the history stays: each rate applies from its date until the next one. To add a rate:

  1. Finance → Currencies, then click “Rates & Edit” on the currency.
  2. Under Exchange Rates, set Applies From (the first day the rate applies, today unless you change it) and the Rate to GHS (cedis per unit).
  3. Click “Add Rate.”

How the forms use the rates:

  • Choosing a currency fills in the rate for the record’s date: a payment’s date, an expense’s date or an invoice’s date. New trips use today’s rate.
  • Change the date and the rate follows, unless you have typed your own rate. Staff can always change the rate on a record, for example to the rate a client actually paid at.
  • If the date is before the currency’s first rate, the field is left empty and staff type the rate.
  • Adding or removing rates never changes saved records: each trip, invoice, payment and expense keeps the rate it was saved with.

Managing the list:

  • “New Currency” adds one, with its code (e.g. XOF), name and, if you know it, its first rate and the date it applies from.
  • On a currency’s page, “Save Changes” updates its name. The code can’t be changed, because saved records use it.
  • Adding a rate for a date that already has one replaces it. “Remove” deletes a rate entered by mistake; the rates around it then apply.
  • “Deactivate” takes a currency off the forms. Records already in that currency keep it. GHS, and the default currency in Settings, can’t be deactivated.
  • “Delete” works only for a currency that nothing uses yet. Once a currency is used, deactivate it instead.

Note

GHS is the base currency, always at 1. Accounts, reports and trip profit are all in cedis, worked out with each record’s own exchange rate. Every rate added or removed is recorded in the Audit Log.

Chapter 12

Reports

Who can do this

Admin and Finance, under Reports.

12.1 The reports#

ReportWhat it shows
RevenueInvoiced revenue by client, by service type and by month
Receivables AgingWhat each client owes, grouped by how long it is overdue: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ days, with the invoices behind each figure
Trip ProfitabilityEach trip’s value minus its expenses, grouped by client, by truck and by month
ExpensesExpenses by category, by truck and by trip
PaymentsPayments collected by method, by client and by account
Fleet UtilizationTrips per truck, with active and idle days
Client StatementOne client’s invoices and payments with a running balance
  • Most reports have “From” and “To” dates. Set them and click “Filter.”
  • “Export CSV” downloads the figures to open in Excel. “Print” prints the page.
  • All amounts are in cedis (GHS).

12.2 Client statements#

Reports → Client Statement: choose the client, set the From and To dates, and click “Generate.” Use the PDF button to download a statement to send to the client. Amounts are in cedis, worked out the same way as the Statement tab on the client page.

Chapter 3

Transport Requests

A transport request is a client asking you to move something: what, from where, to where, and when. Requests come in two ways: staff record them (source “Internal”), or clients submit them in the client portal (source “Portal”). Requests are under Operations → Requests.

Who can do this

Admin and Operations.

3.1 How a request moves#

StatusWhat it meansWhat can happen next
PendingNew, with no quote yetAdd a quote, edit, reject, or delete
QuotedAt least one quote has been sentRevise the quote, accept, or reject
AcceptedThe client agreed to the latest quoteConvert it to a trip
RejectedTurned down, with a reasonReopen it (back to Pending), or delete it
ConvertedA trip has been created from itNothing; follow the trip instead

The “Timeline” on the request page records every change of status, who made it, when, and any comment.

3.2 Recording a request#

  1. Operations → Requests → “New Request.”
  2. Choose the Client.
  3. Optionally choose a Service Type.
  4. Choose the Origin and the Destination. They must be different places.
  5. Enter the Cargo Description, and optionally the Weight (tons), the Preferred Pickup Date and any Special Instructions.
  6. If you already know the price, tick “Add a quote now” and fill in the quote (see section 3.3).
  7. Save. The request gets a number such as REQ-2026-0001 and starts as Pending, or Quoted if you added a quote.

Note

If a location or service type is missing from the list, click “+ Add” next to the field to create it without leaving the form. Only an Admin can add service types.

3.3 Quoting#

On the request page, click “+ Add Quote” (or “+ Revise Quote” once there is one). Enter the Amount, choose the Currency (it starts as the latest quote’s currency, or else the client’s default), set the date the quote is Valid Until (today or later), add any Notes, then click “Save Quote.”

  • The first quote moves the request from Pending to Quoted.
  • You can revise a quote as often as you need while the request is Pending or Quoted. Each revision gets its own number (QUO-…) and the newest is marked “Latest.” The client sees the latest one.
  • When an Admin creates a trip from the request, the latest quote’s amount and currency are filled in as the trip’s value (see section 4.1).

3.4 Accepting or rejecting#

When the client agrees, click “Accept” on the request page. Clients can also accept or reject a quote themselves in the portal (see section 14.2); the timeline then shows “Client (portal)” as the person who made the change.

To turn a Pending or Quoted request down, click “Reject” and enter the reason. A rejected request can be brought back with “Reopen” and a comment; it returns to Pending.

3.5 Editing and deleting#

  • Only a Pending request can be edited. Once it is quoted, change the price with a new quote revision; once accepted, the request is locked.
  • Only a Pending or Rejected request can be deleted.

3.6 Finding a request#

The Requests list can be filtered by status, source (Internal or Portal), client and service type. Click “View” to open a request.

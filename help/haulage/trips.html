Chapter 4

Trips

A trip is the actual job: a truck and a driver carrying a client’s cargo from one place to another. Trips are under Operations → Trips.

Who can do this

Admin and Operations create and manage trips. Finance can open a trip (for example from an invoice or a client) to see its value and expenses and to log expenses, but can’t change the trip itself.

4.1 Creating a trip#

Most trips start from an accepted request:

  1. Open the accepted request and click “Convert to Trip.” The New Trip form opens with the request’s details filled in.
  2. Choose the Truck and the Driver.
  3. Check the Client, Origin, Destination (they must differ), Cargo Description and Weight (tons), and set the Pickup Time if you know it.
  4. Admins also see Trip Value, Currency and Exchange Rate, filled in from the latest quote. Check them.
  5. Click “Create Trip.” The trip gets a number such as TRP-2026-0001 and starts as Assigned. The request becomes Converted.

You can also create a trip without a request: Operations → Trips → “New Trip.”

Note

A truck or driver who is already on another active trip can’t be assigned. When a trip is created, its truck and driver change to “On Trip” automatically.

Caution

Only an Admin can enter a trip’s value. A trip created by an Operations user is saved without one, even when it comes from a quoted request. An Admin should open the trip, click “Edit” and enter the Trip Value before it is invoiced; otherwise the invoice line starts at zero and the trip shows as a loss in the reports.

4.2 Moving a trip through its stages#

On the trip page, the button for the next stage moves the trip forward. You can add an optional comment each time, and the system records the time.

When a trip is completed, its truck and driver go back to Available. Stages can’t be skipped or undone, so check before you click.

4.3 The trip page#

The trip page shows the truck, driver, route, cargo and the recorded times, with four tabs:

  • Overview: the trip details. Admin and Finance also see the Financial Summary: Trip Value, Total Expenses, Profit / Loss, and Invoice Status, with a “Create Invoice” link if the trip hasn’t been invoiced yet.
  • Expenses: the expenses logged against the trip (Admin and Finance only).
  • Timeline: every change of stage, with who made it, when, and any comment.
  • Assignment History: every change of truck or driver.

4.4 Reassigning a truck or driver#

If a truck breaks down or a driver can’t continue, click “Reassign” on an active trip. Choose the new truck and/or driver, and decide for each whether the one you’re replacing should be “Set back to available.” Untick it if, say, the old truck is going for repair. Add a note and save. The change is recorded in the Assignment History.

4.5 Logging trip expenses#

Who can do this

Admin and Finance.

  1. Open the trip and go to the Expenses tab.
  2. Click “+ Log Expense.”
  3. Choose the Account the money came from and the expense Category (e.g. Fuel, Toll, Driver Allowance).
  4. Enter the Amount, the Currency and, for a foreign currency, the Exchange Rate to cedis. Add a Description, the date, and attach a photo or scan of the Receipt if you have one.
  5. Save. The amount is taken off the account’s balance straight away.

To take an expense off, click “Remove.” The amount goes back into the account, and the reversal shows in the account’s ledger.

Note

A trip’s Profit / Loss is its value (converted to cedis) minus its expenses. Log every expense against the right trip to keep the Trip Profitability report accurate.

4.6 Editing a trip and its value#

Click “Edit” on the trip page to correct its details. Only an Admin sees and changes the trip value, currency and exchange rate. Once the trip has departed (En Route and later), the Audit Log records any change to them as a financial override.

4.7 Cancelling or deleting a trip#

  • To cancel a trip that hasn’t been completed, click “Cancel Trip” and enter the reason. Its truck and driver go back to Available.
  • If expenses have been logged against the trip, it can’t be cancelled until an Admin ticks “Override and cancel even if expenses have been logged.” Check the Expenses tab first.
  • A trip can only be deleted if it has no logged expenses and isn’t on any invoice. Otherwise cancel it.

Caution

Cancelling a trip can’t be undone.

Chapter 5

Fleet: Trucks and Drivers

Who can do this

Admin and Operations, under Operations → Trucks and Operations → Drivers.

5.1 Trucks#

Click “New Truck” and enter the Plate Number, Truck Type, Make, Model, Year, Capacity (tons) and any Notes.

StatusMeaningHow it changes
AvailableFree for a new tripAutomatically when a trip ends, or with “Set Available”
On TripAssigned to an active tripAutomatically; you can’t change it by hand
MaintenanceOff the road for repair or service“Set Maintenance”
InactiveNo longer in use“Deactivate”; “Activate” brings it back as Available
  • A truck on a trip can’t be put into maintenance or deactivated.
  • An inactive truck must be activated before its maintenance status can change.
  • A truck can only be deleted if it has never been assigned to a trip. Otherwise deactivate it, which keeps its history.

5.2 Drivers#

Click “New Driver” and enter the Full Name, Phone, License Number, License Expiry and any Notes.

Drivers have the same kinds of status as trucks: Available, On Trip, On Leave (“Set On Leave” and “Set Available”), and Inactive. The same rules apply: a driver on a trip can’t be put on leave or deactivated, and a driver who has ever been on a trip can’t be deleted.

Note

The Drivers list highlights a licence that has expired or expires within 30 days, and the Operations dashboard lists them too. Renew the licence and update the expiry date on the driver’s record.

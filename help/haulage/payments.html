Chapter 9

Payments

Who can do this

Admin and Finance.

9.1 Recording a payment#

  1. Open the invoice. It must be Issued or Partially Paid.
  2. Click “+ Record Payment.” The form shows what is still outstanding.
  3. Choose the Account the money went into.
  4. Enter the Amount, the Currency and, for a foreign currency, the Exchange Rate to cedis.
  5. Choose the Payment Method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Mobile Money, Cheque or Other), and enter the Reference (e.g. a transaction ID), the payment date and any notes.
  6. Click “Save Payment.” The payment gets a number such as PAY-2026-0001.

The account’s balance goes up straight away, and the invoice changes to Partially Paid or Paid by itself.

Note

If a payment is more than the outstanding balance, the form warns you and asks you to confirm before saving the overpayment.

9.2 The Payments list#

Finance → Payments lists every payment, with filters for client, payment method and account. Click an invoice number to open the invoice.

9.3 Deleting a payment#

On the invoice page, click “Delete” next to the payment. The amount is taken back off the account, the reversal shows in the account’s ledger, and the invoice’s status is worked out again.

Caution

Delete a payment only if it was recorded by mistake. To correct one, delete it and record it again with the right details.

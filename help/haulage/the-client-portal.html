Chapter 14

The Client Portal

The client portal is a separate area where your clients sign in to work with you directly. This chapter describes what they see, so you can help them.

14.1 Signing in#

  • Clients go to the portal’s login page: your system’s address followed by /portal/login.
  • They sign in with their email address and the password you set or reset for them (section 7.3).
  • After 5 wrong passwords the login is locked for 15 minutes. After 15 minutes without activity they are signed out.
  • A deactivated portal user sees a message telling them to contact their account manager.

14.2 What clients can do#

PageWhat the client can do
DashboardSee their active trips, their open invoice balance, and quotes awaiting their action
My RequestsSubmit a new transport request, see your quotes, and accept or reject the latest one (with an optional comment)
My TripsFollow each trip’s status and timeline
My InvoicesSee issued invoices with their payments and balance, and download the PDF
Proforma InvoicesSee issued proformas and download the PDF (only if proformas are switched on)
ProfileUpdate their details and change their password

Note

Clients never see draft invoices or proformas, trip expenses, or anything about other clients. Requests they submit arrive in your Requests list as Pending, with the source “Portal.”

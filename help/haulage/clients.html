Chapter 7

Clients

Who can do this

Admin and Finance, under Finance → Clients.

7.1 Adding a client#

  1. Finance → Clients → “New Client.”
  2. Enter the Company Name, Contact Person, Email (each client needs its own) and Phone.
  3. Optionally add the Address, City, Region, Country and Notes, and choose the Default Currency. New quotes for the client start in that currency, but any quote, trip or invoice can use another one if you have agreed it.
  4. Save. The client gets a code such as CLT-2026-0001.

7.2 The client page#

Open a client to see everything about them, on tabs:

  • Overview: company details, Lifetime Revenue (all payments received) and Outstanding Balance (what is still owed on issued invoices).
  • Portal Users: the client’s logins for the client portal (see section 7.3).
  • Requests, Trips, Invoices & Balances, Payments: the client’s most recent records of each kind.
  • Statement: every invoice and payment in date order, with a running balance.

7.3 Portal users#

Portal users are people at the client company who can sign in to the client portal (Chapter 14). A client can have several.

  1. Open the client and go to the Portal Users tab.
  2. Click “Add Portal User.”
  3. Enter their Full Name, Email and a Password (twice), then click “Create Portal User.”
  4. Give them the portal address and their password. They sign in with their email address.
  • “Reset Password” creates a new password and shows it once in a message at the top of the page. Copy it straight away and share it securely: it can’t be shown again. Resetting also signs the user out of the portal.
  • “Deactivate” stops a portal user from signing in, for example when they leave the company. “Activate” lets them back in.

7.4 Deactivating or deleting a client#

A client with trips, invoices or payments can’t be deleted; the message lists what is in the way. Deactivate the client instead: they stop appearing as an active client, and their history stays.

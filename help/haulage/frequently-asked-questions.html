    Appendix A

    Frequently Asked Questions

    I forgot my password. What do I do?#

    Ask an Admin to reset it from Administration → Users. You’ll get a new password and must choose your own when you log in.

    My login is locked. Why?#

    After 5 wrong passwords the login is locked for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or ask an Admin to reset your password.

    I was signed out while working.#

    The system signs you out after 30 minutes without activity, or when your account signs in somewhere else.

    A menu item I expect to see is missing.#

    Menus depend on your role (section 1.5). Proformas also disappear when they are switched off in Settings.

    Why can’t I see the trip value or profit?#

    Operations users don’t see trip values, expenses or profit. Ask an Admin or Finance user.

    A truck or driver isn’t available when I create a trip.#

    They are probably on another active trip, in maintenance or on leave, or deactivated. Check their status under Operations → Trucks or Drivers.

    I can’t put a truck into maintenance.#

    A truck on a trip has its status managed automatically. Complete, cancel or reassign the trip first.

    I can’t cancel a trip.#

    Expenses have been logged against it. Check the Expenses tab; an Admin can override and cancel anyway.

    I can’t delete a trip.#

    It has expenses or is on an invoice. Cancel it instead.

    A trip doesn’t appear when I create an invoice.#

    It is already on another invoice that hasn’t been cancelled, or it belongs to a different client.

    I can’t cancel an invoice.#

    It has payments recorded against it. If those payments were mistakes, delete them first (section 9.3).

    My client can’t see their invoice in the portal.#

    It is still a draft. Issue it (section 8.3).

    Proformas is missing from the Finance menu.#

    An Admin has switched proformas off under Administration → Settings → Proforma Invoices.

    I can’t delete an account, client, truck or driver.#

    It has history the system must keep. Deactivate it instead; it stops appearing in lists and on forms.

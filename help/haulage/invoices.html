Chapter 8

Invoices

Who can do this

Admin and Finance, under Finance → Invoices.

8.1 How an invoice moves#

StatusWhat it means
DraftBeing prepared. Fully editable, and not visible to the client.
IssuedSent to the client and visible in their portal. Only the due date and notes can still change.
Partially PaidSome payment received (set automatically)
PaidPaid in full (set automatically)
CancelledWithdrawn, with a reason

An invoice is overdue when it is Issued or Partially Paid and its due date has passed. Tick “Overdue only” on the Invoices list to see them; Admins also see them on the Dashboard.

8.2 Creating an invoice#

  1. Finance → Invoices → “New Invoice.” (Or click “Create Invoice” on a trip, which starts the invoice with that trip.)
  2. Step 1: Client. Choose the client and click “Next: Select Trips.”
  3. Step 2: Select Trips. Tick the client’s trips to bill. Only trips that aren’t already on another invoice are listed. Then click “Next: Edit Line Items.”
  4. Step 3: Line Items. Each trip becomes a line using its trip value. Edit the descriptions, quantities and prices if needed, and use “+ Add Manual Line” for anything else, such as demurrage.
  5. Check the Currency and Exchange Rate. They start as the first selected trip’s currency (or else the client’s default) and today’s rate from the currency list (section 13.5). Change them if you agreed otherwise. If some trips are priced in another currency, a note reminds you to convert their prices.
  6. Set the Invoice Date, the Due Date (on or after the invoice date) and the Tax Rate (%) if you charge tax. Add any Notes.
  7. Decide whether to tick “Show itemized trip expenses on PDF” (see section 8.4).
  8. Click “Create Invoice (Draft).” The invoice gets a number such as INV-2026-0001.

Subtotal, tax and total are worked out for you, in the invoice’s currency. A draft’s currency and rate can still be changed with “Edit.”

8.3 Issuing, editing and cancelling#

  • Check a draft, then click “Issue Invoice.” The client can now see it in the portal and you can record payments against it.
  • Click “Edit” to change a draft completely, or to change an issued invoice’s due date and notes.
  • To withdraw an invoice, click “Cancel” and enter the reason. An invoice with payments recorded can’t be cancelled.
  • Only a draft can be deleted. Cancel an issued invoice instead.

Note

Once an invoice is cancelled, its trips are free to be billed on a new invoice.

8.4 Invoice PDFs#

The “PDF” button on an invoice offers two versions:

  • Itemized PDF: lists each trip’s logged expenses under its line. Use it when a client has agreed to pay costs such as fuel or tolls at cost.
  • Clean PDF: just the lines, prices and totals.

Clients downloading an invoice in the portal always get the clean version, so internal costs are never shown to them. The PDF uses your organization’s details, logo and invoice footer notes from Settings (section 13.2).

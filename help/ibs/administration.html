Chapter 21

Administration

21.1 Users#

Who can do this

Requires users.manage. A branch-level admin only sees and manages users at their own branch, and can't manage company-wide users.

Creating a user#

  1. Administration → Users → “Add User.”
  2. Enter Full Name and a unique Username.
  3. Choose a Role: Cashier (POS and sales only), Staff (all features, no user management), Admin (full access including user management), or any custom role your business has created.
  4. If your business has multiple branches, choose the user's Branch (one only) and their Visibility: Branch-level (the default — sees only that branch) or Company-wide (sees every branch; only a company-wide admin can grant this).
  5. Set a Password and Confirm Password (minimum 6 characters).
  6. Leave “Active (can log in)” checked, and click “Create User.”

Note

Your plan caps how many active users you can have: 3 on Core, 8 on Growth, unlimited on Enterprise.

Managing an existing user#

  • Edit — change their details, role, branch, or visibility
  • Change Password — sets a new password for them and forces them to log in again
  • Deactivate / Activate
  • Unlock — shown when an account is locked after 5 failed login attempts

21.2 Roles & Permissions#

Who can do this

Requires roles.manage, and the Enterprise “advanced permissions” plan feature (custom roles are not available on Core or Growth).

Administration → Roles lists built-in roles (Admin, Staff, Cashier — these can't be deleted) alongside any custom roles you've created. “+ Add Role” opens a name field and a checkbox grid of permissions grouped by category.

Note

The Admin role can never lose the users.manage or roles.manage permissions — if unchecked, they're automatically re-added on save, so you can't accidentally lock yourself out. A role currently assigned to a user can't be deleted. When building a custom role for anyone who sells, remember to tick “Access Sales / POS.”

Built-in role defaults#

RoleAccess
AdminEvery permission in the system
StaffEverything except Users, Settings, Roles, Branches, Audit Log, Stock Transfers, Back-date Sales, and Edit / Void Sales
CashierPOS and Sales (Access Sales / POS) only — none of the management permissions

21.3 Branches#

Who can do this

Requires branches.manage, and multi-branch enabled on your account (automatic on Enterprise, purchasable as an add-on on Growth).

  1. Administration → Branches → “+ Add Branch.”
  2. Enter the Branch Name and, optionally, Address and Phone.
  3. Click “Create Branch.”

The new branch automatically gets its own Cash account (see section 15.1).

Note

Branches are never deleted, only deactivated (to preserve their history), and the last remaining active branch can't be deactivated.

21.4 Audit Log#

Who can do this

Requires audit.view.

A read-only record of who changed what — voids, permission changes, and edits to users, branches, accounts, and settings. Filter by User, Action, Entity, and a date range; check “Include archived (1+ year old)” to search older history; “Export” downloads the filtered rows (this export is available on every plan). Opening the Audit Log marks the Dashboard's high-risk change alerts as seen.

Note

The Audit Log is branch-scoped like Sales and Purchases history — a branch-level user sees only that branch's entries. System-wide entries, such as role changes, are only visible to company-wide viewers.

21.5 Settings#

Who can do this

Requires settings.manage.

Administration → Settings groups its options into four sections — General, Point of sale, Tax, and Optional features — listed down the left of the page (across the top on a phone); click one to jump to it. Each on/off option is a switch with a one-line explanation under it; click “How this works” for the full explanation. Options that depend on another, such as the tax rate and the expiry options, only appear while it is switched on. Change what you need, then click “Save settings” — the button stays at the bottom of the screen as you scroll.

SettingWhat it does
General → Current planYour plan and its user limit. Upgrading isn't self-service; contact your provider.
General → TimezoneWhat “now” means everywhere — sale and purchase timestamps, reports, exports. Pick where the business operates; the local time is shown under it. Takes effect for everyone from their next page load.
Point of sale → Discount typePercentage or Flat amount, for both cart-item discounts and the order discount beside the POS total.
Point of sale → Start each sale with Walk-in Customer selectedSwitch off if most of your sales go to registered (often credit) customers and you'd rather the cashier always chose one.
Point of sale → Show account balances to all staffSwitch off to hide account balances wherever staff choose an account (POS, purchases, expenses, deposits); only roles with Financial Accounts access keep seeing them.
Tax → Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)Your business's TIN, printed on receipts, goods-received notes, and customer statements. See section 12.5.
Tax → Charge tax on salesCharges tax on POS sales at the Default tax rate shown under it. See Chapter 12.
Tax → Selling and cost prices include taxWhether your prices already include tax, or tax is added on top. See section 12.2.
Optional features → Record truck & driver on sales and purchasesAdds optional truck number, driver's name, and driver's phone fields to the POS, purchases, and direct deliveries. See Chapter 11.
Optional features → Track batches and expiry datesTurns batch and expiry tracking on; the “expiring soon” warning period and whether expired stock can be sold appear under it. See Chapter 10.
Optional features → Sell services as well as productsLets a product be set up as a service, which sells with no stock. See section 8.5.
Optional features → Proforma invoicesGrowth & Enterprise. Adds Finance → Proformas, with how many days a new proforma is valid for and the default terms printed on it. See section 4.6.
Optional features → Keep count of damaged stockKeeps count of damaged goods that may still be sold at a discount, and adds the Damaged Stock report. See section 9.4.
Optional features → Set a different selling price at each branchMulti-branch only. Lets each branch sell a product at its own price; the product's selling price becomes the standard price. See section 8.6.
Optional features → Keep average cost per branchMulti-branch only. Each branch keeps its own average cost, so its purchases only change its own cost and profit. See section 8.7.

Note

If the Tax section, or the truck & driver, services, proforma, or damaged stock switch, is missing, your installation hasn't had that update yet — contact your provider. On the Core plan, Proforma invoices shows as not included. The branch price and branch cost switches only show when your business has more than one branch.

21.6 Subscription#

Administration → 🔑 Subscription shows the status of your subscription and is where a renewal (unlock) code is entered. Any user is taken here automatically if the system is locked.

StatusMeaning
ActivePaid up. Shows the date it's active until and the days remaining.
Renew soonWithin 14 days of expiry. A yellow banner appears on every page; everything still works.
Grace periodExpired, but within the 5-day grace period. Everything still works — renew now to avoid being locked out.
LockedMore than 5 days past expiry. Every page redirects to Subscription until a new code is entered.

Renewing#

  1. Open Administration → 🔑 Subscription and copy the Installation ID.
  2. Send the Installation ID to your provider with your renewal request. The code you receive only works on your installation.
  3. Paste the full code exactly as received (it starts “IBS2-”) into “Enter unlock code” and click “Activate.”

Who can do this

Only users with settings.manage can enter an unlock code. Other users see “Ask an administrator to enter a renewal code to restore access.”

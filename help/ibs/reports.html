Chapter 19

Reports

Most reports can be filtered by a Period preset (Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, This Year, Last Year) or a Custom date range. Reports show your branch; company-wide users see the branch chosen in the header, or every branch with “All branches.” Most reports can be printed, and exported to CSV on Growth+.

If your business sets prices or keeps average cost per branch (sections 8.6 and 8.7), the stock reports use the prices and costs of the branch you're viewing. With “All branches,” each branch's stock is valued at its own price and cost.

19.1 Available on every plan#

ReportWhat it shows
Sales ReportSales in the period, filterable by payment method, payment status, and cashier
Stock ValuationCurrent value of stock on hand
Stock by BranchStock levels of every product, one column per branch plus a total, with average cost and stock value. Filter by Branch, Category, Product / SKU, and Stock Status (In Stock / Low / Out); sort by name, stock, or value; export to Excel.
Top SellingBest sellers by quantity, revenue, or profit, filterable by category and result limit
Low Stock AlertProducts under their reorder level, with severity and days-until-stockout, filterable by category
Expiry ReportOnly while batch tracking is on. Every tracked batch, earliest expiry first: product, branch, batch no., expiry date, days to go, status, quantity, and value at cost. Summary cards for Expired, Expiring within N days, Unknown Expiry, and All Tracked Stock. Filter by status (default: “Needs attention”) and category.
Damaged StockOnly while damaged stock is switched on (section 9.4). Damaged items in stock now, per product, with their value at cost and at the normal price. For a period: how many were marked damaged, sold (with their normal price, what they sold for, and the discount given), returned or written off, and cleared as a mistake. Filter by period and category.
Tax ReportTax charged on sales (output tax) and paid on purchases (input tax) for a period, each by rate, with the sales and purchases that carried no tax, and the Net tax payable (or refundable). Periods: This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, This Year, or a custom range. Print it, or export every taxed sale and purchase — with the customer's or supplier's TIN — to CSV.

19.2 Advanced reports (Growth & Enterprise only)#

On the Core plan, the Advanced Reports add-on adds these reports.

ReportWhat it shows
ReceivablesOutstanding customer debt, with 0/30/60/90+ day aging buckets and risk level. Opening debts are aged from the date they're owed since (section 6.3).
PayablesOutstanding supplier debt, sortable by amount, name, or age, including opening debts (section 7.3). Payments made on a supplier's page count against their oldest debts.
Profit & Loss StatementRevenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating expenses, and net profit for a period, with category breakdown, a daily trend, and comparison to the prior period. Only for those who can see profit (section 19.4).
Dead StockProducts with no sales in a chosen number of days (default 90) that are still holding stock value
Profit MarginsPer-product margin analysis, sortable by margin, profit, or revenue. Only for those who can see profit (section 19.4).
Customer Credit SettlementMulti-branch only. When customers deposit money at one branch and spend it at another, this shows each branch's Deposits Issued, Credit Redeemed, amount already Settled, and Net Position — plus a Suggested Settlement table saying which branch should pay which, and how much.
Branch PerformanceMulti-branch, company-wide users only. Branches ranked by revenue for the period, with sales count, average sale value, gross profit, and margin. Only for those who can see profit (section 19.4).

19.3 Settling customer credit between branches#

  1. Reports → 🔁 Customer Credit Settlement, and choose the period you're settling.
  2. In the Suggested Settlement table, find a row (From branch → To branch, Amount Owed) and click “Record Settlement →.”
  3. On the transfer screen, which only offers the two branches' accounts, confirm the money movement.

Note

A settlement recorded through the report's link is remembered against that period: re-run the report for the same period and it's netted out, so the same amount isn't shown as owed twice.

Note

Profit figures use each sale's cost at the time it was made, so a later purchase at a different price doesn't change the profit already reported for past periods.

Note

Services (section 8.5) count in sales and profit like products, using their cost per service. They have no stock, so they aren't in the stock reports.

Note

Revenue and profit in reports leave out any tax charged on sales — that money is owed to the tax authority. Amounts customers paid and still owe include it.

Note

On a plan without advanced reports, these appear greyed out in the Reports menu marked “🔒 Advanced reports (upgrade).”

19.4 Who sees profit#

Not everyone needs to see how much the business makes. Only these users see its profit:

  • Core and Growth: users with the Admin role, unless your plan has custom roles.
  • Enterprise: users whose role has the View Profit permission (profit.view). The Admin role has it, and you can give it to any other role (section 21.2). This also applies on Growth with the Custom Roles add-on.

Everyone else:

  • Doesn't see Profit & Loss, Profit Margins, or Branch Performance in the Reports menu, and can't open or export them.
  • Sees the Dashboard without today's and this month's profit, and without the profit line on the trend chart. Top Products and Category Performance are ranked by quantity and revenue instead.
  • Sees Top Selling and Stock Valuation without their profit, potential profit, and margin columns, and gets the Stock Valuation, Top Selling, and Dead Stock exports without them.

Note

Product costs, and the margin on a product's own page, are still shown to anyone who can see products. Cashiers land on the POS when they log in, and can open the Dashboard from the menu for its stock alerts. Direct Deliveries still show their markup to anyone who can manage them (Chapter 14).

Chapter 10

Batch & Expiry Tracking

For businesses that sell goods that expire — medicines, food, agro-chemicals — the system can keep each product's stock split into batches, each with a batch number and an expiry date. It's optional and off by default: businesses that don't need it see no change at all.

Who can do this

Turning tracking on requires settings.manage. Choosing which products are tracked requires products.manage. Included in the Growth and Enterprise plans; on Core, available as an add-on.

10.1 Turning it on#

  1. Administration → Settings → Optional features.
  2. Switch on “Track batches and expiry dates.” The expiry options below it appear.
  3. Set “Warn about batches expiring within” — the number of days before expiry that a batch counts as “expiring soon” (default 90, which suits most pharmacies).
  4. Choose what happens to expired stock under “Selling expired stock”: “Don't allow it” (recommended) or “Allow, with a warning.”
  5. Click “Save settings.”
  6. On each product that expires, open Edit and tick “Track batches and expiry dates.” Products you don't tick work exactly as before.

Note

When you start tracking a product that already has stock, that stock becomes one batch with an unknown expiry date. Unknown-expiry stock is treated as the oldest, so it's sold first.

10.2 How tracked stock moves#

When you…What happens to batches
Receive a purchase or Stock InYou enter a batch number (optional) and expiry date (required); the stock is filed under that batch.
Sell at the POSStock comes from the batch that expires soonest; expired stock is refused or sold last, depending on your setting.
Stock OutPick a specific batch (e.g. the expired one) or leave it on “Earliest expiry first.”
Transfer to another branchIn-date stock is sent earliest-expiry first; the receiving branch gets the same batch numbers and expiry dates.
Void a saleStock goes back into the batches it was sold from.
Edit or void a purchaseOnly stock still left in that purchase's batches is changed — stock already sold stays sold.

10.3 Keeping an eye on expiry#

  • Dashboard — the Critical Alerts panel counts expired batches and batches expiring within your warning period, each linking to the Expiry Report.
  • Product list — each tracked product shows its nearest expiry, in red once expired and amber when expiring soon.
  • Expiry Report (Reports → ⏳ Expiry Report) — see section 19.1.

10.4 Turning it off#

Unticking the setting hides batch details everywhere but keeps them; turning it back on picks up where you left off. Unticking a single product stops keeping that product's batches up to date (its batch details are kept in case you tick it again).

Chapter 15

Financial Accounts

Who can do this

Viewing requires financial_accounts.access. Adding a new account additionally requires the Growth+ “manage financial accounts” plan feature (on Core, the Finance Pack add-on).

This is where cash, bank, and mobile money balances live, and how money moves between them.

15.1 Cash, bank, and mobile money accounts#

  • Cash — every branch has its own Cash account (a branch's physical till), created automatically when the branch is created and named after it, e.g. “Kasoa Branch Cash Account.” Cash accounts can't be created by hand or moved to another branch.
  • Bank and Mobile Money — added by you. In a multi-branch business, each can be Company-wide (all branches) or tied to one branch. Only company-wide users can create or edit a company-wide account; a branch-level admin picks one of their own branches.
  • Suspense — the holding account used by the Suspense Account feature (Chapter 18). It isn't offered when choosing an account to pay into or out of.

15.2 Adding an account#

  1. Finance → Financial Accounts → “+ Add Account.”
  2. Enter the Account Name, choose an Account Type (Bank or Mobile Money), and fill in Provider and Account/Phone Number.
  3. In a multi-branch business, choose the Branch: “Company-wide (all branches)” or a single branch.
  4. Enter the Initial Opening Balance.
  5. Click “Create Account.”

Note

Once created, an account's Type and Balance are locked and can't be edited directly — this keeps its transaction ledger consistent. Balance only changes through recorded transactions. A bank or mobile money account's branch can be changed later with ✏ Edit.

15.3 Moving funds between accounts#

  1. Click “🔄 Move Funds.”
  2. Choose a Source Account and a Destination Account (they must differ) and enter the Amount.
  3. Add optional Transfer Charges/Fees and Notes.
  4. Click “Confirm & Move Funds.”

Note

Blocked automatically if the amount is zero/negative or exceeds the source account's balance.

15.4 Account ledger#

“View Ledger” on any account shows its full transaction history, filterable by Type and date range, with a “⬇ Export CSV” option (Growth+).

Note

A branch's last remaining active account cannot be deactivated — each branch always needs at least one place to record money against.

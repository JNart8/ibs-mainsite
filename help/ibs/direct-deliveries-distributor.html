Chapter 14

Direct Deliveries (Distributor)

Who can do this

Requires distributor.manage, and is only available on plans that include the Direct Deliveries feature (typically Enterprise).

Use this for goods that go straight from a supplier's truck to a customer without ever sitting in your own stock — it records the supplier purchase and the customer sale together, in one transaction, without touching warehouse stock levels. Both are recorded at your active branch.

14.1 Recording a direct delivery#

  1. Finance → Direct Deliveries → create new.
  2. Select the Supplier (Outflow) and the Customer (Inflow) — each shows its current balance.
  3. Search and add products; edit Unit Cost, Unit Selling price, and Qty per line — a live Net Margin is shown for each.
  4. If truck & driver recording is on, fill in the 🚚 Truck & Driver fields — they're saved on both the customer sale and the supplier purchase.
  5. Add Transaction Notes if needed.
  6. In the Delivery Summary, set an overall Discount % and Tax % (taken out of the prices or added on top, as for purchases — section 12.2) and review Sale Subtotal, Purchase Subtotal, Final Sale (Customer), Final Purchase (Supplier), and Net Estimated Profit.
  7. Set the Customer Payment (method — including Deposit — amount paid, and Inflow Account) and the Supplier Payment (method, amount paid, Outflow Account).
  8. Click “🚚 Record Distributor Delivery.”

Note

Customer payment follows the same rules as the POS: Deposit draws at most the customer's deposit balance (the rest is left owing); a customer holding a deposit can't take the sale on credit; and the sale can't take the customer past their credit limit.

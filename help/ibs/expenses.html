Chapter 17

Expenses

Who can do this

Requires expenses.manage and the Growth+ “expenses” plan feature (on Core, the Finance Pack add-on).

17.1 Recording an expense#

  1. Finance → Expenses → “Record Expense.”
  2. Choose the Expense Date and the account to Pay From.
  3. Pick an existing Category or type a New Category.
  4. Enter a Description/Particulars and the Net Amount.
  5. Add optional Transaction Charges and Notes.
  6. Click “Record & Pay Expense.”

Note

Blocked if the amount is zero or the paying account doesn't have enough balance. Deleting an expense afterward reverses the deduction and restores the account balance automatically.

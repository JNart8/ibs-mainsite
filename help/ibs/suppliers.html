Chapter 7

Suppliers

Who can do this

The Suppliers module requires the suppliers.manage permission.

7.1 Adding a supplier#

  1. People → Suppliers → “+ Add Supplier.”
  2. Fill in Company/Business Name (required) and Phone (required).
  3. Supplier Code is generated automatically if left blank.
  4. Fill in whichever of Contact Person, Alt Phone, Email, TIN (see section 12.5), City/Location, Credit Limit, Address, and Notes are relevant.
  5. Click “Save Supplier.”

7.2 The supplier detail page#

Shows how many products this supplier provides and their credit limit, with buttons for “+ New Purchase” (pre-fills this supplier on the Create Purchase screen), “💳 + Payment,” and Edit.

Note

A supplier with purchase history isn't deleted — it's deactivated instead, using the toggle on their profile, so past purchase records stay intact.

A day in your business, with IBS Business Management

From opening up to the end of the month, the same system follows every cedi and every carton.

  1. 7:45

    Open the dashboard

    Low stock, batches close to expiry and customers who are overdue are waiting at the top, before the first sale. Click through it

  2. 10:30

    Sell at the counter

    Scan or search, then take cash, mobile money, bank or credit. Account customers can't go past their credit limit, and money they paid in advance is used first. Click through it

  3. 13:00

    Receive a delivery

    Record the supplier's invoice with last time's cost already filled in. Batch numbers and expiry dates go in with the stock, and your average cost updates itself. Click through it

  4. 18:00

    Close the till

    See today's sales by cash, mobile money and bank, what each account holds, and what is still owed.

  5. Month end

    Know your numbers

    Profit and loss, expenses, the tax report for your VAT return, who owes you and who you owe, and the stock that isn't moving. Click through it

Made for how you trade

Pharmacies and chemical shops

  • Batch number and expiry date recorded on every delivery.
  • The till sells the earliest-expiring stock first, and can refuse expired stock.
  • An expiry report shows what to return to suppliers while you still can.

Wholesalers and distributors

  • Credit limits, deposits and statements for every account customer.
  • What you owe each supplier, and part-payments against it.
  • Goods delivered straight from supplier to customer, with the truck and driver recorded.

Built in Accra, for business in Ghana

Cedis and mobile money

Prices in GHS, and mobile money is a payment like cash, with its own balance you can check at any time.

Tax handled properly

Charge VAT inside your prices or on top. TINs print on receipts and goods-received notes, and the tax report totals what you charged and what you paid.

You decide who does what

Cashiers see the till; managers see the money. Every voided sale, edit and permission change is kept in an audit log.

Your data, kept apart

Each business runs on its own installation and database, never mixed with anyone else's.

IBS Haulage, for transport and haulage companies

From a client's transport request to the paid invoice, every trip, truck and driver in one system.

Requests and quotes

Clients send transport requests, you reply with a quote, and an accepted quote becomes a trip.

Trips, trucks and drivers

Dispatch trips, assign trucks and drivers, and follow each trip's status from loading to delivery.

Invoices and payments

Invoice completed trips as PDFs, record payments, and send clients their statements.

A client portal

Clients log in to request transport and follow their own trips, invoices and statements.

Money and expenses

A cash book across your accounts, transfers between them, and expenses by category.

Reports on every trip and truck

Revenue, what clients owe and for how long, how busy each truck is, and the profit on every trip.
